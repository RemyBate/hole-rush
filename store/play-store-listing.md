## Full description (max 4,000 characters)

```
Hole Rush is a colorful arcade game for mobile. Drag a hungry hole across each level, collect food before the timer runs out, and unlock new stages as you improve.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag anywhere to move the hole
• Collect food to grow and raise your score
• Complete mission goals before time runs out
• Finish levels to earn coins and star bonuses
• Use boosters from your inventory on tougher stages

FEATURES
• One-finger controls — easy to learn, satisfying to master
• Short levels built for quick play sessions
• Progress saved automatically on your device
• Lives, coins, boosters, and level unlocks
• Music, sound, and vibration controls in Settings
• Portrait mode designed for phones

CHALLENGE YOURSELF
Plan your path, pick the right food, and beat the clock. Can you clear every level with a perfect run?

Hole Rush is free to play. The game works offline and does not contain advertisements. Progress is stored locally on your device.

Support: remymbu12@gmail.com
```
