# Privacy Policy — Hole Rush

**Last updated:** June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Hole Rush** (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile game on Android devices.

If you have questions, contact us at **remymbu12@gmail.com**.

---

## 1. Summary

Hole Rush is an offline arcade game. We do **not** require an account, do **not** show advertisements, and do **not** sell your personal information.

Most data stays **on your device** to save your game progress and settings.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Data stored on your device (local save data)

The App saves game-related information locally so you can continue playing. This may include:

- Game progress (current level, unlocked levels, scores)
- In-game currencies and items (coins, keys, gems, lives, boosters)
- Audio and gameplay preferences (music, sound, vibration, notifications toggles)
- Reward and progression values related to gameplay

This data is stored in files on your device (for example, under the App’s private storage area). It is **not** transmitted to our servers because the App does not use network connectivity for gameplay or account services.

### 2.2 Information we do not collect

We do **not** collect:

- Name, email address, or phone number (unless you contact us voluntarily)
- Precise location
- Contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or SMS
- Payment or financial information (the current version has no in-app purchases)
- Advertising identifiers or analytics profiles

---

## 3. How we use information

Local save data is used only to:

- Restore your progress and settings
- Provide core App functionality (levels, boosters, lives, coins, etc.)
- Apply your preferences (such as sound and vibration)

We do not use this data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

---

## 4. Sharing of information

We **do not** share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

---

## 5. Third-party services

The current version of Hole Rush does **not** integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or social login SDKs.

If we add third-party services in a future update, we will update this Privacy Policy and, where required, the Google Play Data safety section.

---

## 6. Permissions

The App may request the following device permission:

- **Vibration** — used for optional haptic feedback when enabled in Settings

The App does not request Internet access in the current release.

---

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Local save data remains on your device until:

- You clear the App’s data in Android settings, or
- You uninstall the App

Uninstalling Hole Rush removes locally stored game data from your device.

To request help deleting data or ask privacy questions, email **remymbu12@gmail.com**.

---

## 8. Children’s privacy

Hole Rush is a casual game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Parents and guardians should supervise younger players’ use of mobile devices and app installations.

---

## 9. Security

Because data is stored locally on your device, its security also depends on your device settings (screen lock, OS updates, etc.).

We do not transmit gameplay save data over the network in the current version.

---

## 10. International users

If you use the App outside your home country, your local data remains on your device subject to this Policy.

---

## 11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above.

Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

Material changes may also be communicated through the App or the Google Play store listing where appropriate.
